Kaggriculture Round11 research revision — 24 September 2026

This package preserves user-supplied benchmark agents and the user-supplied
kaggriculture-cppsim source for reproducing the reported local experiments.
They are not newly authored policies, and inclusion is not a claim of ownership,
endorsement, universal simulator equivalence, or official Kaggle performance.

The original notices within inputs/agents/*.py and engine_cppsim/ are retained.
The agents credit their respective upstream contributors, including Thomas
Tschinkel, Ahmed Berat Ozer, shiiin9, Dmitrii Gluzdov, Yusuke Hayashi and other
contributors named in the original source. Preserve those notices when creating
or redistributing derivatives. The full Apache License 2.0 is supplied as
THIRD_PARTY_APACHE_2_LICENSE.txt and within engine_cppsim/LICENSE.

The newly written diagnostic overlays are in scripts/probe_game.py, not silently
patched into the original benchmark inputs. They are development experiments,
not promoted submission agents. scripts/executed/ retains the scripts actually
run in this session, including their original environment paths. The portable
scripts at scripts/ have path and output handling adjustments, and the panel
runner includes an additional implementation-fingerprint cache guard.

Public-source references in SOURCE_REGISTRY.json distinguish original authors,
readable public mirrors, executable artifacts and author-reported measurements.
Not all referenced candidate agents are present or have been executed here.

The reports and result tables distinguish newly run simulations, inherited
analyses, external reports and unexecuted proposals. No new model was trained
and no Kaggle submission was made during this research revision.
