# V100 (rejected, not a submission candidate)

V100 applies the V90 candidate-relative teacher ranker only when its score gap
is at least 0.16 and its chosen task is among V14's three best individual-cost
tasks. V99 episode-held-out imitation showed high precision inside that narrow
region, but V100 closed-loop tests did not generalize.

The known-seed group improved mean reward by 12,459.17. A new three-seed,
two-seat group regressed by 5,812.67 on average and by 26,798 in the worst pair.
All games completed and no animal loss was observed, so the remaining failure
is a long-horizon economic and global-assignment externality. V100 is retained
for research and must not replace V14 by default.
