# Kaggriculture V118

V118 is a 3000-rating-target research candidate derived only from V117 and the
repository-owned V111 runtime chain. A rating of 3000 is an objective, not a
claim or guarantee.

The two interventions are deliberately bounded. A unit standing on a weed digs
only when the inherited action is mechanically certain to do nothing. During
steps 590–599, Carrot seeds may be bought if no inherited purchase shares the
turn, at least 500 coins remain, the Carrot price is at least 47, and the gross
four-Carrot yield exceeds the six-Wheat yield by at least 20 coins. During
steps 600–699, inherited Wheat plantings are changed to Carrot only under the
same price gate and when every changed actor has a seed available.

All movement, watering, harvesting, land, labor, and livestock actions remain
the parent policy. The design addresses random weed desynchronization and adds
late price-responsive crop diversity without importing the unsafe or
license-unverified complete-policy candidates studied on 2026-09-14.

Evaluation on four paired games against V117 was effectively neutral
(1 win, 1 loss, 2 draws; mean margin +12.25). V118 is therefore retained as a
license-clean fallback, not promoted as the strongest candidate.
