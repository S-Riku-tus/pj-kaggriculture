# Kaggriculture V43

V43 tests a geometry hypothesis from episode-level V11 and Top-3 replay
analysis. On Day 6, V11 puts about 5.89 crops and 2 empty pastures in the first
eight newly unlocked central cells. Rank 1 puts 3 crops, 1 pasture, and 3.98
empty cells; Rank 2 puts 1.54 crops and 6.42 empty cells. By Day 9, Rank 1 and
Rank 2 convert 4.50 and 3.84 of these cells to animals, versus 2.03 for V11.

The candidate relocates only PLANT tasks from two NE cells in the common
Rank-1/2 animal core to the nearest legal outer cells on Days 4-8. It does not
reduce planned crop count or alter market orders. The rule is disabled until
closed-loop mean, lower-tail, maintenance, and animal-loss tests pass. No
rating improvement is claimed.
